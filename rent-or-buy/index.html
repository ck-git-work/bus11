What stays the same
  • Price $700,000, 20% down. Matt has $154,000 (down payment plus closing costs).
  • Mortgage: 7.3% fixed for 30 years ($3,839 a month).
  • Owner’s other costs: property tax $745, HOA $545, insurance $50 a month, growing 2.5% a year; maintenance 1% of the home’s value a year.
  • Rent: $3,677 a month to start for a similar unit, growing 3% a year.
  • “The difference” = the owner’s monthly cost minus rent. It starts near $2,100 and shrinks as rent rises. When rent passes the owner’s cost, both renters pay the extra from their investments.
  • Ignores taxes on investment gains, the mortgage interest deduction and the cost of selling the home.
