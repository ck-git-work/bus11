What this class is

My goal is for you to leave with the confidence to make the right financial decisions for you and the people you care about. The class will be hands on, interactive, and, I hope, very practical. Over the next twelve weeks we will explore questions such as:

  • How do I take inventory of my finances and make a budget I will actually keep?
  • What is the right way to use credit day to day? How do I use it for big purchases like a car or a home?
  • When and how do I start investing?
  • How do I handle taxes, insurance, and retirement planning without getting lost?
  • What tricks does my mind play on me around money?
  • And does money buy happiness?

How class works

In-person meetings: a 20 minute lecture on the week's topic followed by a case study that explores a real-world personal finance question. Case studies will be done in small groups but you'll submit your own copy in Canvas with a short reflection question. I will supplement the in-class sessions with asynchronous material, usually video, that will cover parts of the topic we didn't manage to get to in-person or that will cover topics where the class would benefit from further explanation. You will also complete two projects this quarter that you'll choose from a list of possible projects.
